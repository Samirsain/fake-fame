Spoken lines for the quiz (see lib/voice.ts)

Made with HeyGen text-to-speech (starfish engine) through the media-use skill's heygen-tts.mjs, then trimmed and
loudness-normalised with ffmpeg (mono, 24 kHz, mp3). Hinglish uses the Hindi clips.
  Hindi voice:   "Young Yumna"  (c76a2a4b9976410aa76a1c5f57f38c78), female, --lang hi
  English voice: "Cecily - Warm & Friendly" (41f17b4ebd334ac99a91c2aada5f86d7), female
Check HeyGen's terms for your plan before commercial use of generated audio.

hi/ and en/ hold the same file names:
  ok1, ok2      right answer
  bad1, bad2    wrong answer
  start         "let's go" when the player taps start
  tier0..tier3  result: Fake friend, Sus, Real one, Bestie (friends quizzes only)

  tier0 Fake friend! Who even are you? Blocked.   tier1 Sus. You're on thin ice.

To change a line (needs `npx hyperframes auth login` once):
  node ~/.claude/skills/media-use/audio/scripts/heygen-tts.mjs "<line>" --voice c76a2a4b9976410aa76a1c5f57f38c78 --lang hi -o line.wav
  ffmpeg -i line.wav -af loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11 -ac 1 -ar 24000 -c:a libmp3lame -q:a 4 hi/<name>.mp3
